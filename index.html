Questions parents ask

Good to know

How many students are in a batch?

We keep batches to a maximum of 8 students so each child gets real individual attention.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. There is no fee and no commitment. It's a chance for your child to meet the teacher and for us to understand their needs.

Which boards do you cover?

We teach to CBSE, ICSE and state board syllabi. Tell us your child's board when you enquire.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. Online and in-person options are both available. Ask us which suits your child's schedule.

How will I know my child is improving?

You'll receive test results regularly and a monthly progress update, and you're welcome to speak with the teacher any time.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the class level and subjects. Send us an enquiry and we'll share a clear fee sheet with no hidden charges.
